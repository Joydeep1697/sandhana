Formal Definition

IUPAC / ISO 80000-5

In thermodynamics, entropy is a state quantity measuring the thermal energy unavailable to perform mechanical work during a thermodynamic process.

In statistical physics, it constitutes a logarithmic measure of the number of microscopic configurations (Ω) compatible with a defined macroscopic equilibrium state.
