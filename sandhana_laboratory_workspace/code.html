Scientific Interpretation

The simulation demonstrates that quadratic air resistance breaks the spatial symmetry of Galileo's parabolic arc. Because drag force scales with v², maximum energy dissipation occurs during initial high-speed ascent, causing the descent path to be noticeably steeper than the ascent phase.
